# VisitCoach: pitch for Maharashtra

Slide content for the state pitch (MENTOR.md #14). Every number has a source in [MENTOR.md](MENTOR.md) section 3. Demo numbers from our made-up data are **not** quoted as results.

> **One line:** VisitCoach gives Maharashtra's mentors more time to mentor: every classroom visit ends with one clear, NIPUN-aligned action for the teacher, sent on WhatsApp, and the next mentor picks up where the last one left off.

---

## Slide 1: The problem

**Mentoring visits happen, but the coaching doesn't carry over.**

- A Kendra Pramukh observes a Grade 1–2 class, talks with the teacher, then writes it up. The writing takes time away from mentoring.
- The teacher gets spoken feedback, and often nothing to keep.
- **Mentors change.** The next mentor doesn't know what was agreed last time, so every visit starts from zero, and the teacher can't keep track either.
- Classes are often **multi-grade**. Maharashtra has about **8,150–9,270 single-teacher schools** (UDISE+ 2024-25 / 2025-26), where all grades sit together, so general advice doesn't fit.

## Slide 2: What the state already asks for

> The supervision system should give **"support, counselling, facilitation and mentoring"**, not inspection.
> (*Pragat Shaikshanik Maharashtra*)

> The Kendra Pramukh **"is expected to help teachers to plan a teaching programme that ensures that not a single child is left behind."** (*Pragat Shaikshanik Maharashtra*, p.1)

- The state's own **KP Academic Skill Matrix** (KPALP, CETE TISS 2023) describes the expert Kendra Pramukh as one who **"gives clear and actionable feedback"** and **"regularly records and tracks the progress of all teachers"**, with **"follow-up … regular and timely"**. VisitCoach is a tool for reaching exactly that level.
- **NIPUN Maharashtra** now measures about 34 lakh children every month, and *"underperforming schools receive mentoring visits within days, not months"* (VOPA evidence document, 2026). The measurement exists. **What happens in the mentoring visit is what's still missing.**
- The state already has the people (Kendra Pramukhs) and a checklist (KPALP classroom observation).
- **What's missing is the follow-through between visits.** That's what VisitCoach adds.

## Slide 3: One visit, start to finish (the demo)

1. **Before:** Sachin, the new Kendra Pramukh, opens Sunita's page. Sachin sees Sunita's **coaching history**: three visits by two earlier mentors, what each one agreed, and whether it happened. This opens with no signal.
2. **In class:** Sachin taps a short reading/maths checklist next to the KPALP form, notes *32 children, Grades 1 and 2 together*, and records 30-second voice notes in Marathi. Everything is saved on the phone offline.
3. **After:** in about a minute, Claude writes one page of coaching in Marathi. It **builds on the earlier mentor's action** and picks a practice that works for two grades at once. Sachin edits and approves. **No report to write.**
4. **To the teacher:** Sunita gets a **WhatsApp message in Marathi** with what went well, one thing to work on and the agreed action. The link shows the full 5-minute practice and **Sunita's coaching so far**.
5. **Next visit:** whoever comes checks the action ("done / partly / not done"), and the next advice builds on it.

> From our real test packet (31 Oct): *"Paired reading has started, good progress. But today only the front rows did it… pair each back-row Grade 1 child with a Grade 2 reader."* It picked up the earlier mentor's action, noticed an older action was already done, and fitted the advice to a multi-grade class.

## Slide 4: Built for how Maharashtra works today

| What the state has | How VisitCoach fits |
|---|---|
| Kendra Pramukhs visit and observe | Same visit, same talk with the teacher; just no write-up afterwards |
| KPALP observation checklist | Our checklist is a **reading and maths add-on**, not a replacement: every item shows the KPALP indicator it adds detail to (e.g. *"Children read text themselves" → KPALP 2.1 Student engagement*). It's a data file, so the state can swap in its own |
| Teachers asked for training in **multi-grade** teaching (Pragat Shaikshanik: 1.82 lakh training requests, "mainly for constructivist, multi grade, maths and language") | The mentor records which grades sit together, and the practice is built to work across them |
| NIPUN Bharat / NIPUN goals (Lakshyas), Pragat Shaikshanik, KPALP | Every piece of advice cites the page it comes from: 31 checked excerpts, 10 of them from Maharashtra documents |
| Teachers on WhatsApp: **73%** use it; **98%** use EdTech, **83%** have a device in school (BSET 2025, Maharashtra) | The teacher gets the advice **in WhatsApp**. No app, no login |
| **67%** of teachers report internet problems (BSET 2025) | The mentor's app works fully offline and syncs later |
| Mentors change | The coaching history travels with the teacher, not the mentor |

**Mentoring, not monitoring:** the block view is titled *"Support planning: what training does this cluster need?"*. It shows counts by need ("14 of 22 schools: children reading on their own"). It never shows teacher names or scores.

## Slide 5: Cost, proof and the ask

- **Cost:** about **₹10–12 per coaching packet** (AI and speech-to-text).
- **What a pilot would measure:**
  1. time from observation to feedback in the teacher's hands
  2. % of visits that end with one concrete action
  3. % of actions done by the next visit
  4. how often mentors edit the AI's draft
  5. how useful teachers find the advice (1–5)
- **The ask:** a **one-cluster pilot** (about 20 schools) with SCERT Maharashtra / Samagra Shiksha Maharashtra for one term, using the state's KPALP checklist next to ours.
- **Phase 2:** a WhatsApp bot, so mentors can also send voice notes with no app at all. It reuses the same speech-to-text, AI coaching and history.

## Speaker notes

- Lead with the story (slide 3), not the tech. The judges and the state care that **Sunita's coaching doesn't restart every time the mentor changes**.
- If asked "isn't this surveillance?": the teacher sees everything recorded about them; the block sees counts only, groups under 5 schools are merged, and nothing is scored.
- If asked about other states: Hindi and Tamil already work (checklist, packets, teacher page). Maharashtra is the first state, not the only one.
